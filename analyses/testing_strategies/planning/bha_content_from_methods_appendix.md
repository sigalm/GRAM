## TabCAT Brain Health Assessment (BHA)

The TabCAT Brain Health Assessment is a cognitive screening tool used to
identify individuals with MCI or dementia [@possin2018]. It produces a
cognitive score, and the addition of an optional informant (care partner)
survey produces a global score. The test performance measures for both are
reported below based on a -1.5z cutoff (Source: Elena Tsoy).

**BHA Cognitive Score (BHACS)**:

| **Comparison** | **Parameter** | **Sensitivity** |
|----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Prodromal CI vs. Healthy | `sens_BHACS[1]` | 0.44 |
| Memory loss vs. Healthy | `sens_BHACS[2]` | 0.48 |
| MCI vs. Healthy | `sens_BHACS[3]` | 0.66 |
| Dementia vs. Healthy | `sens_BHACS[4]` | 0.96 |
| **Specificity** | `spec_BHACS` | **0.93** |

**BHA Global Score (BHAGS)**:

| **Comparison** | **Parameter** | **Sensitivity** |
|----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Prodromal CI vs. Healthy | `sens_BHAGS[1]` | 0.56 |
| Memory loss vs. Healthy | `sens_BHAGS[2]` | 0.61 |
| MCI vs. Healthy | `sens_BHAGS[3]` | 0.84 |
| Dementia vs. Healthy | `sens_BHAGS[4]` | 0.98 |
| **Specificity** | `spec_BHAGS` | **0.92** |

### Screening Interventions

The base model does not include screening (`test = NULL`). Screening
scenarios are configured separately by defining test parameters,
eligibility criteria, frequency, and stop rules.

Beyond the BHA result itself, GRAM tracks the broader diagnostic pathway an individual moves through once tested, as well as diagnoses that occur independently of BHA screening.

**Selection for testing (`SELECT`)**: When a screening scenario is active, selection for testing is drawn from age- and status-specific probabilities (`scenario$probs_select`), with an optional relative risk of being selected again for individuals selected at their previous assessment (`rr.select_prior`). What selection represents depends on the strategy: spontaneous cognitive concern under reactive testing, an EHR-based risk flag under selective testing, or a random opt-in under inclusive testing.

**Observed CDR-SB (`CDR_obs`) and severity (`SEV_obs`)**: For individuals who screen positive on the BHA, an observed CDR-SB score is generated by adding rater measurement error to the true CDR-SB (`r.CDR_sd3`; currently set to 0, i.e. no measurement error modeled). Observed severity is then derived from the observed CDR-SB using the same cutoffs as the true severity classification.

**Diagnosis under standard care (`DX`)**: Captures clinical diagnosis that can occur independently of BHA screening, reflecting empirical underdiagnosis of MCI and dementia. Conditional on having a regular healthcare provider, an undiagnosed individual with true cognitive impairment is diagnosed in a given cycle with assumed probability 0.15 (MCI), 0.40 (mild dementia), or 0.85 (moderate/severe dementia). Once diagnosed, an individual is no longer eligible for BHA testing.

**Primary care provider (PCP) follow-up**: For screening scenarios that include a PCP confirmation step, BHA-positive individuals may receive a PCP follow-up assessment that acts as an additional filter — correctly confirming true positives with assumed probability varying by severity (`p.PCP_confirm_TP` = 0.75/0.88/0.95/0.98 for MCI/mild/moderate/severe) and correctly rejecting false positives with assumed probability `p.PCP_reject_FP` = 0.65.

**PET and neuropsychological assessment (`PET`, `NP`)**: Attributes exist to represent confirmatory PET imaging and specialist neuropsychological assessment, but are not yet fully developed — their update functions currently pass the prior cycle's value through unchanged, with no testing logic implemented.
